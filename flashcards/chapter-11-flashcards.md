## Chapter 11: Billing, Pricing, and Support: Cost Management Tools and Support Plans — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | Business Support: response time for 'production system down'? | Under 1 hour. Exam tip: Business is the minimum plan offering this 1-hour SLA. |
| 2 | Enterprise Support: response time for 'business-critical system down'? | Under 15 minutes — fastest SLA tested on the exam. Enterprise On-Ramp offers under 30 minutes for the same case type. |
| 3 | Which support plans get FULL Trusted Advisor checks (all 5 categories)? | Business, Enterprise On-Ramp, and Enterprise. Basic and Developer only get 7 core checks (mostly security + service limits). |
| 4 | AWS Cost Explorer forecast window? | Up to 12 months into the future, based on up to 12 months of historical data. Exam tip: Cost Explorer visualizes/forecasts — it does not alert. |
| 5 | AWS Cost and Usage Report (CUR) — what makes it unique? | Most granular billing dataset (hourly/daily/monthly line items) delivered to an S3 bucket. Used for custom BI/analytics, not built-in visualization. |
| 6 | AWS Budgets — 4 budget types? | Cost budgets, Usage budgets, RI utilization/coverage budgets, Savings Plans utilization/coverage budgets. Exam tip: Budgets = proactive alerts; Cost Explorer = after-the-fact analysis. |
| 7 | Which support plan includes a single designated Technical Account Manager (TAM)? | Enterprise only. Enterprise On-Ramp gives access to a pool of TAMs, not a dedicated one. |
| 8 | AWS Basic Support plan — what's included? | 24/7 access to customer service, docs, whitepapers, and 7 core Trusted Advisor checks. No technical support cases. Free for every AWS account. |
| 9 | Developer Support: response time for 'system impaired'? | Under 12 business hours. General guidance is under 24 business hours. No 1-hour or 15-minute SLAs exist below Business tier. |
| 10 | What is AWS re:Post? | Free community Q&A platform (replaced AWS Forums) where AWS experts and community members answer questions. Not a formal paid support channel. |
| 11 | AWS Trusted Advisor's 5 check categories? | Cost Optimization, Performance, Security, Fault Tolerance, Service Limits. Memory trick: 'C-P-S-F-S'. |
| 12 | How do you enable detailed cost tracking by tag in Cost Explorer/CUR? | Activate cost allocation tags in the Billing and Cost Management console — tags won't appear in reports until activated. |
