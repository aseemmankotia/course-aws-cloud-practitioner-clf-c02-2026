| 1 | CLF-C02 exam: total questions and total time? | 65 questions in 90 minutes. Exam tip: budget ~1.2-1.3 min/question to leave a buffer for review. |
| 2 | How many of the 65 CLF-C02 questions are unscored? | 15 unscored questions, mixed randomly and indistinguishable from scored ones. Exam tip: answer every question fully — you cannot identify which ones don't count. |

| 4 | Pearson VUE test center vs Pearson OnVUE online proctoring — key logistics difference? | Test center: staffed physical location, no environment check needed. OnVUE: remote exam requiring private room, ID verification, and webcam scan of the workspace before starting. |

| 6 | What does the 'flag' feature in the AWS exam interface do? | Marks a question for later review while allowing the candidate to submit a current best-guess answer and continue. Exam tip: never leave a question fully unanswered — there is no penalty for guessing. |
| 7 | Strategy for scenario questions with two seemingly correct answers? | Re-check the scenario's stated constraints (cost, time, compliance, scale) — eliminate the option that ignores any constraint, even if technically accurate. |
| 8 | How should time be reallocated if behind pace mid-exam (e.g., 15/65 done at 30 minutes)? | Speed up: flag difficult questions with a quick best guess instead of deep analysis, to ensure all 65 are attempted before time runs out. |
| 9 | S3 storage class for rarely accessed data needing millisecond retrieval? | S3 Standard-IA. Exam tip: use Glacier tiers only when retrieval delay of hours-to-days is acceptable. |
| 10 | AWS Support plan offering 24/7 phone/chat/email with guaranteed response times but no TAM? | Business Support. Exam tip: Enterprise (On-Ramp/Enterprise) adds a Technical Account Manager on top of these features. |
| 11 | Review workflow at the end of the CLF-C02 exam — what should be prioritized in the final buffer minutes? | Revisit only flagged questions first, not already-confident answers. Exam tip: reserve last ~10 minutes strictly for flagged-question review. |
| 12 | Is there a penalty for guessing on the CLF-C02 exam? | No penalty for incorrect answers. Exam tip: always submit an answer before time expires, even a guess, rather than leaving it blank. |
