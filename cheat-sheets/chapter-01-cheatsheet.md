## Chapter 1 Cheat Sheet: Cloud Value Proposition

### On-Premises vs Cloud
| Model | Spending | Capacity | Lead Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| On-Premises | CapEx, large upfront | Fixed, often over-provisioned | Weeks–months to procure |
| AWS Cloud | OpEx, pay-as-you-go | Elastic, matches demand | Minutes |

### The Six Advantages (exam order)
1. Trade capital expense for variable expense
2. Benefit from massive economies of scale
3. Stop guessing capacity
4. Increase speed and agility
5. Stop spending money running/maintaining data centers
6. Go global in minutes

**Exam trap:** Questions often give a scenario and ask you to name the SINGLE advantage — pick the most specific match, not a broader one.

### Elasticity vs Scalability vs Agility
| Term | Meaning | Keyword |
|---|---|---|
| Elasticity | Auto add/remove capacity in real time | "automatically scales in and out" |
| Scalability | Ability to grow (sometimes manual) capacity | "can handle growth" |
| Agility | Speed to provision/experiment/launch | "rapidly develop and launch" |

### Cost Avoidance vs Cost Savings
| Term | Definition | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Cost Savings | Reduction in actual current spend | Lower monthly bill vs on-prem |
| Cost Avoidance | Preventing a future cost | Skipping a hardware refresh cycle |

### AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) — Six Perspectives
| Category | Perspectives |
|---|---|
| Business Capabilities | Business, People, Governance |
| Technical Capabilities | Platform, Security, Operations |

- **Business:** ensures IT aligns with business needs/ROI
- **People:** staff readiness, culture, org change
- **Governance:** managing risk, compliance, skills to run cloud strategy
- **Platform:** architecture patterns, provisioning solutions
- **Security:** confidentiality, integrity, availability of data/workloads
- **Operations:** running, monitoring, managing workloads day-to-day

**Exam trap:** People vs Governance — People = staff/culture; Governance = risk/compliance skills.

### Global Reach → Reduced Time to Market
- Deploy to new AWS Regions in minutes instead of building data centers (years)
- Directly supports advantage #6 ("go global in minutes")

### Common Exam Traps
- Confusing Elasticity (automatic, both directions) with Scalability (general growth capability)
- Assuming Cost Avoidance = a reduced bill (it's actually a prevented future expense)
- Mixing up CAF perspectives — memorize as 3 Business + 3 Technical
